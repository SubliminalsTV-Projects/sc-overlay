## Option A — with a role ping

SC Overlay 0.1.44 is out, and <@&Notify - SC Overlay> this is the one that fixes blueprints if you don't play in English.

If Star Citizen wasn't running in English — or you use one of the community language packs — the app was recording few or none of your blueprints, and nothing on screen told you. A German client resolved 0 of 475 pool blueprints. One pack missed all 166 of its entries. It reads your game's own language file now and translates back, so it works for every language and every pack, including ones that don't exist yet. Measured after the fix: German 475 of 475, all three packs at 100%.

The tracker also stopped sitting empty between contracts. It now shows the pools you're closest to finishing, what each one pays, and where you stand with your givers — written as contracts to your next rank rather than raw rep, because "5,300 rep to Prestige 2" doesn't tell you if that's an evening or a month and "about 27 contracts" does.

Also in this one: a search box so you can look up any contract without alt-tabbing, mission info that finally tells you how long a contract takes and when you can retake it (that cooldown isn't the number the game shows you), hotkeys for any widget instead of the four that happened to have them, and a fix for hiding Twitch Chat or Web Page mid-typing eating your clicks on every monitor.

It'll update itself next time you open it.
