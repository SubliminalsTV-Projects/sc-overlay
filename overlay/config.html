Collection & privacy
iBacks up your blueprint collection + tracked mission to your subliminal.gg account and lets you browse it on the web. Needs a token — get one at subliminal.gg/blueprints → “Connect the desktop tracker” and paste it below.
iSends your Star Citizen Game.log so mission and blueprint parsing can be fixed against real logs. Your handle, account ID, IP and session are stripped and chat is dropped before it leaves your PC. Needs a sync token.
Screen reading (OCR)
Uses Windows' built-in text recognition, only while Star Citizen is focused.
iReads the tracked-mission panel so the overlay knows which mission you've actually selected — ground truth the game log can't give. No account needed.
iWhen you open a Fabrication Kiosk in-game, the app snaps the item's picture and contributes it to subliminal.gg's catalog — only items we don't have yet. You're credited on the contributor leaderboard. Needs a sync token.
iThe game only writes a blueprint to your log the moment you receive it, and those logs get deleted over time — so anything you earned before installing the app can never be detected. The Fabrication Kiosk only lists blueprints you own, so when it shows one we have no record of, you get 30 seconds to add it. You confirm each one, so check the name matches what's on your screen.
Widgets & interaction
iShows the Mining Scanner on its own when the scanner or Refinement Center is detected. Only a signature matching a known ore counts — debris and stray numbers won't pop it open. If you close it, it stays away for a bit before it can pop again.
iWith this on, the overlay is click-through during gameplay — hold the interact key (set it under Hotkeys) and hover a widget to click it. Prevents accidental clicks mid-game.
Infographic Viewer
iPick a PNG (transparency supported — e.g. a joystick binding chart) and it fills the Infographic Viewer, which you place and size like any other. Toggle it with the binding hotkey (set under Hotkeys) or from the tray.
Setup & defaults the essentials, sensible out of the box
Game log
iPick the game.log FILE itself (not its folder) — e.g. …\StarCitizen\LIVE\game.log. Browse… to select it, or Detect to auto-find your install and channel (LIVE / PTU / …).
Appearance
iRestyle the overlay to a Star Citizen manufacturer. Mobiglas is the clean default; Drake is rugged burnt-orange; Anvil is military teal-green; Grey's is grungy CRT green; Auto matches the ship you're flying. Applies immediately. (Per-widget sizing: drag a widget's corner in arrange mode.)
Auto (matches the ship you're flying) and Mobiglas are free. 🔒 Pinning one manufacturer regardless of ship is a subscriber perk — pick one to preview it, then it reverts. Subscribe any way below and it stays unlocked for as long as you're subscribed: Twitch → SubClub → subliminal.gg →
Applies to Auto theme and the stream ship-signal (/api/ship). Off = keep the last ship's manufacturer skin until you board another.
Hotkeys
iClick a button then press your combo. Use Ctrl/Alt/Shift + a key, or a bare F3 / F13–F24 (those are free in Star Citizen; F4–F12 are taken). Applies immediately, no restart.

Graphics & performance
Hides the on-screen overlay while mission tracking and sync keep running — handy if you just want it out of the way. If you are turning this off because the overlay is crashing your game, please report it — that is a bug worth fixing, not something to work around.
iOn an AMD GPU and the game crashes (Vulkan “device lost”) when the overlay is up? Turn this on — it forces the overlay off the GPU with a simpler no-blur panel. Requires restarting the app.
iOff by default — the overlay renders on the CPU, avoiding crashes some GPUs hit. Turn on only if you have GPU headroom and no crashes. Requires restarting the app.
Fade while you play Off
How visible the overlay is while you are not focused on it. It returns to full the moment you click or Alt-Tab to it. 100% turns the fade off.
Full opacity (hold-off) key
Snaps the overlay back to full opacity without leaving the game — press again to let it fade.

About

    

      SC Overlay is a lightweight, transparent in-game HUD for Star Citizen. It reads your
      game.log to show the blueprint reward pool for the mission you're tracking and checks off each
      blueprint as you collect it — plus a Mining Scanner (signature scanner + refinery-job timers)
      and manufacturer skins that automatically match the ship you're flying. Opt-in screen reading
      (OCR) can detect your tracked mission in-game and help crowdsource item images for the catalog. Point it at your
      game.log below to get started, and optionally connect your
      subliminal.gg account to sync your collection and
      browse everything you've unlocked on the web.
