### New

- **Look up any contract without leaving the game** — There is a magnifying glass in the top right of the Mission & BP Tracker. Type part of a contract's name, pick it out of the results, and you get the same brief the panel shows for a mission you are actually running — the pool, what it pays, who gives it, how long it takes. You do not have to have accepted it, and you do not have to alt-tab to the website to look something up mid-session. Press Escape or the ✕ to close it and hand the keyboard back to the game.
- **The tracker has something to show you between contracts** — The tracker used to sit empty until you accepted something. Now, when you are between contracts, it shows you the blueprint pools you are closest to finishing — with what each one pays, how long a run takes, and the aUEC per hour that works out to — plus where you stand with your mission givers and the blueprints and missions you saw most recently. Standing is written as the number of contracts to your next rank rather than a raw reputation figure, because "5,300 rep to Prestige 2" does not tell you whether that is an evening or a month, and "about 27 contracts" does. The mission picker has an Idle option now too, so you can get back to this screen once you have accepted something.
- **The fastest route to your next rank, worked out for you** — The Event Tracker keeps the reward ladder exactly as it was and adds, underneath it, the quickest way to actually reach your next rank. Three contracts ranked by reputation per hour, each with its run length, what it pays, and how many runs and how much total time it would take to close the gap you are on. It is scoped to the next rank rather than the whole track on purpose — the best rep per hour in the abstract is trivia, but "this one, seven runs, about an hour and ten" is a plan. The spread is usually much wider than people expect: on one real track the top row was four times the rate of the bottom one.
- **How long a contract takes, how hard it is, and when you can retake it** — Mission info now carries how long a contract actually takes to run, how hard it is, whether it can be retried if you fail it, and how long after finishing before you are allowed to take it again. That last one is the wait most people care about and it is not the number the game shows you — a contract can run 12 minutes and still lock you out for 45 after you hand it in. The completion card picked up the run length too. This comes from the same per-contract dataset the website has been using for weeks, trimmed down to only what the app draws so it costs 0.38 MB rather than the full 5.95 MB.
- **A hotkey for any widget you want one for** — Every widget can have a hotkey now, not just the four that happened to have one. There are no defaults and there deliberately never will be — eleven preset key combinations would collide with each other, with Star Citizen, and with whatever you have already bound. Every row starts out reading "None — click to set", so nothing changes until you decide it should. Any hotkeys you had set before still work exactly as they did.

### Improved

- **The report card says where a payout figure came from** — The after-action card used to print whatever payout figure it had, so a calculated estimate looked exactly like a reward the game actually handed you. Now the figure is labelled with where it came from, ranked the same way the website ranks it: a payout logged live reads "aUEC", one reported by other players reads "reported", one straight out of the game files reads "payout", and a modelled guess reads "~N estimated". Estimates are also no longer added into your earnings totals — roughly 2,000 contracts have modelled payouts, and counting those as money made the totals fiction.
- **The info buttons open when you click them** — The circled i beside a figure used to only respond to hovering, which meant clicking it — the obvious thing to do — appeared to do nothing at all. It now opens on a click as well. Hovering still works exactly as before.
- **When something goes wrong, one paste now tells us what** — Restarting the app used to destroy the log covering whatever just went wrong, which is unfortunate given that restarting is the first thing anyone tries. One previous log is now kept. On top of that, errors in the overlay's own interface are recorded where before they vanished silently, and Copy diagnostics now carries that recent history and the tail of the log along with it — so one paste in Discord has what we need instead of three rounds of questions. Sync and Twitch tokens are stripped out by value before anything is copied. There is an Open logs folder button next to it.
- **The mission facts are in a sensible order** — The row of facts on a mission is in a deliberate order now rather than whatever order the code happened to build them in. One consequence worth mentioning: Reputation and Rank moved out of the faction group and into the main row, so that group now shows the faction and your standing bar only.

### Fixed

- **Blueprints were never recorded if you did not play in English** — If Star Citizen was not running in English, or you use one of the community language packs, the app was recording few or none of your blueprints — and nothing on screen said so. Your pools just stayed dark while you ground them out. The game writes its notifications in whatever language you play in, so the app was reading text it did not recognise: a German client resolved 0 of 475 pool blueprints, and one pack renamed things in a way that missed all 166 of its entries. Pack users had it worst, recording no blueprint receipts at all. The app now reads your game's own language file and translates back to the names it knows, which works for every language and every pack, including ones that do not exist yet. It only ever reads that file. Measured after the fix: German 475 of 475, and all three packs at 100%.
- **Your standing no longer vanishes on certain contracts** — Accepting certain contracts made your standing with that faction disappear from the panel entirely, which looked like the app had lost your reputation. It had not — the standing was tracked correctly the whole time. Some contracts pay into a reputation track the app does not rank, and rather than falling back to your overall standing with that giver it showed nothing at all. This is not rare: 384 of 4,075 contracts pay only unranked tracks, and for Headhunters it is 88 of them, which is most of their board.
- **Hiding a widget mid-typing no longer eats your clicks** — Hiding the Twitch Chat or Web Page widget while you were typing in it left the overlay holding the keyboard with nothing on screen to explain it — the overlay would eat clicks on every monitor until you restarted. Both widgets now release the keyboard when they are hidden, the way the notepad and chat already did.
- **The tracker tells you when it cannot identify a blueprint** — If your language file could not identify a blueprint, the pool simply stayed dark, which reads as the app being broken rather than as a name it does not know. The tracker now says so, and shows the exact text the game wrote so you can see why. Where a modified language file is involved there is a Recalibrate button that re-reads it and goes back over blueprints already recorded under names it could not place, so it fixes the pool you are looking at rather than only future receipts. Settings has a card showing which language file is in use and how many names it resolved.
